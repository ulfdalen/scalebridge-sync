Let's get your scale talking to Garmin

This app watches your Withings account for new measurements and copies them into Garmin Connect on a schedule. Setup takes about five minutes, most of it spent in the Withings developer portal — which is the part everyone gets stuck on, so we walk you through every field.

Everything runs and stays on this computer.
No cloud, no account, no telemetry. Your measurements go from Withings to this machine to Garmin, and nowhere else. The only things ever written to disk are your settings and the access tokens needed to talk to those two services.
What you'll need
  • Your Withings account — the same one your scale already reports to.
  • Your Garmin Connect email and password (and your phone or inbox, if Garmin asks for a code).
  • About five minutes, and this computer left on afterwards so the sync can run.
One slightly odd part, up front

In the next step you'll create your own free Withings developer application. That sounds heavier than it is: it is a web form with about five fields, it costs nothing, and no human reviews it.

Your application is yours alone: your data flows directly between Withings, this computer and Garmin — it never passes through anyone else's server.

Rather not run anything yourself? The hosted version at scalebridge.ulf.su does all of this in the cloud — connect once and forget it exists.

Get this exactly right

Somewhere on the application form there is a field called Callback URI (sometimes "Redirect URL"). It tells Withings where to send you after you approve access. It must be this address, character for character:

  • http, not https — this address is on your own machine, so there is no certificate.
  • localhost, not 127.0.0.1 — Withings treats them as different strings.
  • It includes the port, :8723. Dropping it is the second most common mistake.
  • It ends in /callback.
  • No trailing slash after callback.
  • Paste it — don't type it. One transposed character and Withings will refuse to send you back.

The goal is a matched pair of strings that prove to Withings that requests are coming from your application. They may be labelled Client ID / Client Secret, Consumer Key / Consumer Secret, or API key and API secret. Both are normally 64 lowercase letters and digits. If the secret is only shown once behind a "reveal" button, copy it now — regenerating it is possible but means coming back here.

Let this app read your measurements

Now Withings asks you whether your own application may see your data. The consent page opens in a new tab. Approve it there, then come back here — this page checks every couple of seconds and moves on by itself.

Sign in to Garmin Connect

Garmin has no developer portal for this, so the app signs in the same way their mobile app does. If your account has two-factor authentication turned on, Garmin will send a six-digit code and we'll ask for it next.

How much history should we bring over?

Old measurements are uploaded in batches after setup finishes. Anything Garmin already has is skipped, and you can re-run this later from Settings — so there is no wrong answer here, only a slower one.

How often should it check?

Each check is one small request to Withings, and nothing is uploaded unless something new turned up. You can change this at any time in Settings.

You're set

The first sync is already running. From here on, this app checks Withings on its own and quietly pushes anything new into Garmin Connect.
